## Statement
Every finite-dimensional linear subspace of a normed space (Definition~\ref{def:banach}) is closed.

**Established result; proof not yet included.** This is standard finite-dimensional normed-space theory, used for example in \cite[solution to Exercise 2.4]{belton}. The ambient space need not be complete.
