## Statement
In a metric space as in Definition~\ref{def:complete}, a subset $A$ is *nowhere dense* if $\interior(\overline A)=\varnothing$. It is *meagre* if it is a countable union of nowhere dense sets. It is *residual* if $X\setminus A$ is meagre. All closures and interiors are taken in the ambient space $X$. A *Baire space* is a space in which every countable intersection of open dense sets is dense.
